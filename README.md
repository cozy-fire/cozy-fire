## Current Research

### Cross-Task Learning with Continuous Thought Machines

I am currently investigating the cross-task learning capabilities of **Continuous Thought Machines (CTMs)**, an architecture introduced in *Continuous Thought Machines* ([arXiv:2505.05522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05522)).

This project examines whether CTMs can learn multiple tasks sequentially while retaining knowledge acquired from earlier tasks.

**Research objectives:**

1. Evaluate the cross-task training capabilities of CTMs.
2. Investigate catastrophic forgetting during sequential training.
3. Explore how CTM's architectural characteristics influence knowledge retention and transfer.
4. Compare experimental behaviors with established continual learning approaches.

The experiments are ongoing, and I am working toward a reproducible research report. ([repo link](https://github.com/cozy-fire/continuous-thought-machines))
